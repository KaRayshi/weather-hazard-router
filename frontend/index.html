Key capabilities

What STORMLINE does

A risk-averse routing engine that fuses live weather feeds with road network data to keep you safe.

🌧️

Live Weather Integration

Pulls real-time precipitation, visibility, wind speed, and temperature from the Open-Meteo API every 5 minutes and maps readings to nearby road segments via geofencing.

🛣️

Risk-Aware Routing

Uses a modified A* algorithm with weather-weighted edge costs. The risk-aversion slider lets you trade off travel time against safety — from “shortest path” to “avoid risk at any cost.”

📊

3 Route Alternatives

Generates Fastest, Balanced, and Safest routes using an edge-penalization technique, then ranks them with plain-language explanations backed by real weather data.

🟢

Color-Coded Hazard Map

Every road segment is color-coded green / yellow / red based on its computed weather risk score. Hazardous segments pulse to draw your attention.

⚠️

Hazard Threshold Alerts

When a segment’s risk score exceeds the critical threshold, the system triggers a hazard warning and can automatically suggest a reroute around the danger zone.

🛡️

Historical Fallback

If the live weather API goes down, STORMLINE falls back to historical flood and drainage data so routing never goes blind. The outage is surfaced clearly in the UI.

Workflow

How it works

From loading the map to getting your verdict — in four steps.

Pick your area

Pan the map to your area of interest. STORMLINE pulls real road geometry from OpenStreetMap via Overpass.

Set origin & destination

Tap two points on the map or search by place name. The system loads roads and weather data for the corridor in between.

Weather is evaluated

Open-Meteo readings are geofenced to each road segment. A weighted risk formula calculates each segment’s score (rain, visibility, drainage, flood history).

Get your verdict

STORMLINE shows three alternative routes — Fastest, Balanced, and Safest — ranked with clear weather-backed reasoning and drawn on the map in distinct colors.
